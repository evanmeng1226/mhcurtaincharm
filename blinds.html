Modern Custom Blinds.

Sleek Architectural Lines, Effortless Light Control, and Everyday Durability.

Designed for versatile light management and crisp modern aesthetics, our custom blinds offer space-saving solutions for every room in your home.

Explore our extensive range—from UV-blocking sunscreen and 100% blockout roller blinds to classic timber and moisture-resistant faux wood venetians, as well as energy-efficient cellular honeycomb shades.

Engineered for smooth daily operation and built with high-grade, low-maintenance materials, our blinds are available in both manual and whisper-quiet motorized options.

Aluminum Venetian Blinds.


Bring modern clarity, exceptional light control, and long-lasting durability to your interior spaces with our Premium Aluminum Venetian Blinds. Engineered with precision-formed, high-grade aluminum slats, these classic window coverings offer a clean, tailored aesthetic that complements both residential interiors and commercial architecture.


Whether you need total privacy, controlled natural sunlight, or a sleek moisture-proof solution for high-humidity areas, our Aluminum Venetian Blinds deliver reliable daily performance with low maintenance.

Zebra Blinds Collection.


- Light Filtering Series: Gently diffuses sunlight to reduce glare while keeping rooms bright and welcoming.


- Blackout Series: High-density woven fabric layers provide up to 95%+ room darkening and maximum privacy.


- Cordless Safety Series: Completely eliminates hanging pull cords. Effortlessly adjust by raising or lowering the bottom rail by hand.


- Smart Motorized Series: Built-in quiet motor compatible with Alexa, Google Assistant, and mobile apps for automated scheduling and remote control.


- Textured & Moisture-Resistant Series: Premium woven linen textures or water-resistant fabric coatings designed for high-humidity areas.


Roller Blinds Collection.


- Solar Screen Series: Cuts harsh solar glare and heat while maintaining an open view to the outside during daylight hours.


- Translucent Light Filtering Series: Softens incoming sunlight into a warm, diffused glow while blocking outside line-of-sight into your interior.


- Total Blackout Series: Multi-layer opaque backing blocks 100% of incoming light and provides maximum privacy day or night.


- Dual Roller Series: Combines two independently operating shades (Solar Screen + Total Blackout) on a single compact dual-bracket mechanism.


- Smart Motorized Series: Whisper-quiet Lithium-ion rechargeable motors controlled via remote, smartphone app, or voice assistants (Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, Apple HomeKit).


Roman Blinds Collection.


- Flat Fold Series: Features a smooth, uninterrupted panel when closed that folds into neat, flat horizontal pleats when raised.


- Hobbled Series: Designed with cascading fabric folds that maintain a soft, romantic wave effect even when fully extended down.


- Thermal Blackout Series: Backed with a high-density thermal blackout lining that blocks 100% of external light, prevents draft leaks, and reduces ambient street noise.


- European Soft-Sag Series: Crafted without rigid horizontal dowels, allowing the fabric to dip gently in the center for an elegant, unconstructed drape.


- Smart Motorized Series: Integrated whisper-quiet motor allows effortless raising and lowering via handheld RF remotes, smartphone apps, or voice commands (Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit).


Honey Combshades Collection.


Engineered for superior climate control and clean contemporary aesthetics, our Honeycomb Shades Collection (also known as Cellular Shades) features an innovative honeycomb cell structure that traps air in insulating pockets. This natural thermal barrier keeps interior spaces significantly cooler in summer and warmer in winter, cutting energy bills while delivering precise light control.


Whether you want to diffuse harsh daylight in a living room, block out 100% of light in a bedroom, or maintain privacy while preserving overhead sunlight with a Top-Down Bottom-Up design, our Honeycomb Shades provide a quiet, elegant solution for modern living.
